Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024
Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. We are committed to being transparent and keeping your information safe.

Introduction

Online Shop ("we," "us," or "our") operates the online marketplace at this website. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or make a purchase from us.

By using our services, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please discontinue use of our services.

1 Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services to you:

Personal Info

Name, email, phone, billing & shipping address

Payment Data

Card type & last 4 digits (never full card number)

Order History

Products purchased, amounts, and order status

Usage Data

Pages visited, clicks, session duration, device info

Location Data

IP-based country/city for shipping & fraud prevention

Communications

Messages you send via contact forms or support chat

2 How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders, including payment processing and shipping
  • Creating and managing your account and preferences
  • Sending order confirmations, shipping updates, and receipts
  • Providing customer support and responding to your inquiries
  • Sending promotional emails and newsletters (only with your consent)
  • Analyzing website usage to improve our products and services
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud and technical issues
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our terms

3 Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with trusted partners only as described below:

  • Shipping partners — to deliver your orders (name, address, phone)
  • Payment processors — to handle transactions securely (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Analytics providers — to understand website usage (anonymized where possible)
  • Email service providers — to send transactional and marketing emails
  • Legal authorities — when required by law or to protect our legal rights
We never sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers. All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

4 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our site. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cart, login session, security tokens Session
Analytics Page views, traffic sources, user behavior (Google Analytics) 2 years
Marketing Personalized ads, retargeting campaigns 90 days

You may opt out of non-essential cookies at any time by updating your browser preferences or using our cookie consent banner.

5 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • SSL/TLS encryption on all data transmitted to and from our site
  • PCI-DSS compliant payment processing — we never store full card numbers
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls limiting who can access your data internally
  • Automatic session timeouts for inactive accounts

Despite these measures, no internet transmission is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized use of your account, please contact us immediately.

6 Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account remains active
  • Order records are kept for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Marketing data is deleted upon unsubscribing
  • Analytics data is anonymized after 26 months

7 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you

Rectification

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data

Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")

Opt-Out

Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time

Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

Restriction

Request that we limit how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@onlineshop.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8 Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.

9 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

10 Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date, or by sending you an email if the changes substantially affect your rights. Your continued use of our services after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
