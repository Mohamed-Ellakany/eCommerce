Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our store, orders, shipping, and more.

Browse our catalog, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or log in to your account for a faster experience. You'll receive a confirmation email once your order is placed successfully.

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number on our website under "My Account → Orders" or on the courier's website to track your shipment in real time.

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and changes may not be possible. Please contact our support team as soon as possible if you need to make changes.

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express delivery takes 1–3 business days. Same-day delivery is available in select cities. Estimated delivery time is shown at checkout based on your location.

Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $140. Orders below this threshold have a flat shipping fee depending on your location. During sales events, free shipping thresholds may be reduced.

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping times vary between 7–21 business days depending on the destination. Additional customs duties and taxes may apply and are the buyer's responsibility.

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags attached. Sale items and personal care products are non-returnable. To start a return, go to My Account → Orders → Request Return.

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The amount will be credited to your original payment method. Bank processing times may add an additional 2–5 days.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption for your safety.

Absolutely. We never store your full card details on our servers. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We use industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption to protect all data transmitted on our platform.

Click "Sign Up" in the top navigation bar. Fill in your name, email, and password. You'll receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account. You can also sign up using your Google or Facebook account.

Click "Login" then select "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email and we'll send you a password reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact our support team.
